💙 A little gift for Chuti 💧

Your very own water pet, sent with love. One step.

1Double-click DeskPet.exe. A little water pet appears, already connected to its friend. Nothing to install, nothing to sign in to. If Windows asks whether you trust it, choose More info and then Run anyway.
Little tips. Right-click your pet to change its Avatar, Size or Opacity. You can also drag any picture onto the pet to make it a new avatar. Holding Ctrl and rolling the mouse wheel over the pet changes its size. Messages from your friend appear as a small, quiet bubble: click it to hide it, or right-click and choose Recent messages to read them again. Just type to chat. To give the pet a command, start with its name, like Bubbles, time, or with a slash, like /time.

Your pet and its friend find each other with a secret code, so there is nothing to connect. If your friend is offline, your message waits and arrives when their pet wakes up. Messages are scrambled so only the two of you can read them.
