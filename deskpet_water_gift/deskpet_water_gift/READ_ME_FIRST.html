💙 A little gift for you 💧

Your very own water pet, sent with love. Follow these 5 small steps and it will pop up on your screen.

1Install Python. Go to python.org/downloads, download it, and when the installer opens, tick the box “Add python.exe to PATH” before you click Install.
2Install Tailscale. Go to tailscale.com/download, install it, and sign in with a Google or Microsoft account. This is the little private tunnel that lets your pet talk to its friend.
3Open the invite link and press Accept:
https://login.tailscale.com/admin/invite/KWrEM3MZf347RNLcjxmt11
4Open the folder “deskpet_water_gift” and double-click start.bat. The first time it sets itself up, which takes about a minute.
5Say hi! A little water pet will appear and wander around your screen. Double-click it to type a message and it will carry it to its fire friend. Right-click it for the menu, and choose “Start with Windows” so it always stays with you.

If it can’t connect, the fire pet’s computer might be switched off. The water pet keeps trying by itself, so it will connect as soon as the fire pet is online. Everything stays between the two computers: no accounts, no servers, no API keys.
